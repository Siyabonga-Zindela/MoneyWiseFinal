Common scam warning signs

These patterns appear again and again in financial scams in South Africa. One sign alone does not prove a scam — but several together are a strong signal to walk away.

Upfront fees to release money

“Pay a release fee / clearance fee / tax first.” Legitimate lenders and lotteries do not ask you to pay to receive your own winnings or loan.

Guaranteed, outsized returns

“Double your money in 10 days, zero risk.” Every real investment carries risk. Guaranteed high returns are a hallmark of Ponzi and pyramid schemes.

Pay to recruit others

Earnings that depend on signing up friends and family — rather than selling a real product — are the core mechanic of pyramid schemes.

Pressure to act fast

“Offer expires today”, “pay within 10 minutes”. Scammers rush you so you cannot verify or ask advice. Urgency alone is not proof of a scam, but it is a reason to slow down.

Requests for PINs, passwords or OTPs

No bank or legitimate company will ever ask for your PIN, password or one-time PIN — not by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp.

Hard-to-reverse payment methods

Gift cards, cryptocurrency transfers or cash to a stranger are favourites of scammers because the money is almost impossible to get back.

Security and privacy

How your information is actually handled — no vague promises.

No account needed to check

The scam checker is open to everyone. If you create a free account, the reports and checks you file are linked to it so the review team can follow up with you — and admins and external partners sign in to their own portals.

What is stored

Messages you check, and the resulting analysis, are stored so that the evidence trail and any linked report can be reviewed. Please do not include PINs, passwords or ID numbers in anything you paste or report.

How it is protected

Account passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Login sessions use short-lived signed tokens. IP addresses are stored only as salted hashes, never as raw values. Sensitive endpoints are rate-limited.

Your POPIA rights

Account holders can export everything held about them or request correction or deletion through the built-in POPIA privacy requests. Report screenshots are stored privately and are not served publicly.

Terms of use, in short

MoneyWise is an awareness tool, not a financial adviser, regulator or law-enforcement agency. Results are automated opinions based on the text you provide and known warning-sign patterns; they can be wrong in both directions. Do not rely on a low-risk result as proof that an offer is safe. If you have lost money to a scam, contact your bank immediately and report it to the South African Police Service and the FSCA.
